### description

Open every safe cell. Each number counts the mines around it, and every dig should follow from a number. DIG is minesweeper played the way it was meant to be played: by deduction.

NO GUESSING WHERE IT COUNTS

Patch (9 by 9) and Field (16 by 16) boards are checked before you see them: the two rules of the game clear them from the first open area without a guess. Quarry (16 by 30, 99 mines) is too dense to verify and says so.

A HINT THAT TEACHES

Press Hint and the board outlines a cell the numbers already prove safe. When nothing is provable, the hint says so, and the only move left is a guess you know is a guess.

A LOSS THAT EXPLAINS

Dig a mine and the card tells you which number had already proved it, or that the guess was forced. Your stats keep the two apart.

THREE SIZES AND A DAILY

Best times per size go to Game Center. A new Field board every day, the same for everyone, with a streak.

LEARN THE IDEA

A five-step tour: the goal, what a number means, flags, one worked deduction, and a practice board. The idea screen shows the board as a system of equations and lets you apply every proof at once.

DIG has no ads and no subscription, and nothing in the game is locked. There is one optional support purchase. It pays for maintenance and gives you a supporter mark, the Dusk colors, a warm background setting, and early TestFlight builds when they are available.

DIG is open source. Made for iPhone and iPad by Michell Zappa at Centaur Labs.
