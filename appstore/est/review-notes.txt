EST can be played without an account. On first launch, tap Skip if the
tutorial appears. App Review can test Solo 81, Quick 27, and one-phone Duel
without signing in. Game Center is optional and is used for leaderboards and
two-device matchmaking.

The app has no ads or subscriptions. It includes one optional, one-time
non-consumable support purchase. It unlocks no gameplay: the supporter mark
and optional cosmetic finish are purely cosmetic, and every game mode and
feature is available without payment. App Review can test the app without
buying it. The purchase is available from Settings > Support EST and can be
tested with the included StoreKit configuration.

The app collects no personal data unless a player chooses to give it. Send feedback has an optional email field, used only to reply. Anonymous diagnostics are enabled by
default on new installs and can be disabled in Settings; in a configured
release build, they send one coarse aggregate record per week with activity
totals such as games
started, games completed, and sets found, and never includes per-game events or
identifiers. Settings also includes a privacy-thresholded Community Pulse
summary from the first-party endpoint. Forks can disable or replace that
endpoint. The source code and privacy policy are
available at:
https://github.com/michellzappa/games
