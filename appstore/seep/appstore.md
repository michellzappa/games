### description

One color at a time, flood the whole board. The flood starts in the top-left corner. Tap a color and every cell touching the corner takes it, then the region grows into every neighbor of that color. When one color covers the board, you are done.

SEEP is a free puzzle about looking ahead. Each board has a par, the number of moves a steady player needs, and a limit of par plus three. Finish at par for three stars, one over for two, any finish for one. Undo takes a move back. A hint shows the strongest next color, and a hinted board keeps its stars but stays off the leaderboard.

THREE PACKS

• Pool: 10 by 10, four colors. Thirty boards to learn the shape of a good move.
• Lake: 14 by 14, five colors. Thirty boards where one greedy move costs you par.
• Ocean: 18 by 18, six colors. Thirty boards that reward planning two moves out.

Every board is the same on every device, so a friend's Lake 12 is your Lake 12.

TODAY'S BOARD

One new board a day, the same for everyone. Keep the streak going.

LEARN THE IDEA

A five-step tour shows the goal, one move, par and stars, why a move fails, and a practice board. The idea screen goes further: watch the flood search the board one ring at a time, see the greedy strategy, then run an exact search on a small board and find where greedy falls short.

MAKE IT YOURS

Pick a color theme, turn on color-blind assist for a symbol on every cell, and turn sound and haptics off when you want quiet.

SEEP has no ads and no subscription, and nothing in the game is locked. There is one optional support purchase. It pays for maintenance and gives you a supporter mark, the Dusk colors, a warm background setting, and early TestFlight builds when they are available.

SEEP is open source. Made for iPhone and iPad by Michell Zappa at Centaur Labs.
